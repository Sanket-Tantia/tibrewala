Privacy Policy & Terms of Use

This Privacy Policy and Terms of Use governs the TE Connect mobile application, developed and published by TE Lights.

Effective Date: November 15, 2026 Application: TE Connect Package: com.teconnect.remote

01. Information Collection and Storage

TE Connect is engineered with an offline-first architecture. We do not operate remote servers to collect, store, or monitor your personal data or lighting usage:

  • No Account Required: You are not required to create an account, log in, or provide personal credentials, email addresses, or phone numbers to use the application.
  • On-Device Data Storage: All room configurations, fixture identifiers, custom presets, schedules, and sleep timers are stored strictly on your local device.
  • No Cloud Tracking: We do not operate tracking servers or cloud databases that monitor your space configurations, daily schedules, or hardware activity.

02. Device Permissions

The application requests only permissions strictly necessary for local radio communication and timing functionality:

  • Bluetooth Nearby Devices (BLUETOOTH_SCAN, BLUETOOTH_ADVERTISE, BLUETOOTH_CONNECT): Used exclusively to broadcast Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) control packets directly to compatible fixtures. In accordance with Android API standards, this permission declares neverForLocation. The application does not collect, track, or share your geographic location.
  • Exact Alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM): Required to schedule precise morning wake-up routines and sleep countdown timers.
  • Foreground Service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_CONNECTED_DEVICE): Ensures that active sleep countdown timers run reliably without premature termination by device battery management.
  • Network State (INTERNET): Used exclusively when you tap the official TE Lights brand banner to open our corporate website (tibrewalaelectricals.com) in your external device browser. The application itself performs zero background network data transfers.

03. Zero Third-Party Advertising & Zero Data Collection

TE Connect is strictly an offline-first utility application:

  • No Third-Party Ad Networks: The application does not integrate Google AdMob, third-party advertising SDKs, tracking frameworks, or analytics services.
  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not collect, process, or transmit Google Advertising IDs (AAID), device fingerprints, or ad interaction telemetry.
  • Direct Brand Display: The app displays an internal, first-party brand banner for TE Lights linking directly to our official website (tibrewalaelectricals.com). This internal banner does not track users, store identifiers, or set cookies.
